Privacy Policy

App: CRIA HV — Intravenous Fluids

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Controller: Amanda Machado · CRM-RJ 52.116305-1 · @cria_saude

Contact: amanda.l.machado@hotmail.com

Summary

This app collects no personal or health data, stores nothing on servers, and transmits nothing. Everything you type stays on your device and is gone when you close the app.

There is no login, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and no sharing with third parties. The only sign-up that exists is optional and happens outside the app — see Optional mailing list, below.

What you type

Weight, volumes, concentrations and other clinical values are held in memory only, on your device, to produce the result shown on screen. They are never written to storage, never leave the device, and cannot be recovered after the app is closed.

No patient data is recorded. The app never asks for a name, date of birth, chart number or any other identifier, and provides nowhere to enter one.

Optional mailing list

The app offers an invitation — never required and never on launch — to leave your name, email, role and institution in an external form that opens in your device's browser.

It exists for two purposes only: to tell you when new calculators are released, and to invite professionals to take part in the clinical validation study of this calculator.

  • The app fills in nothing and sends nothing: you type into the form, outside the app
  • Nothing you enter in the calculator reaches the form
  • Filling the form in or ignoring it changes nothing about how the app works
  • The form is hosted by Google Forms; responses are held in a spreadsheet under Amanda Machado's control
  • To leave the list or have your entry deleted, write to the contact at the foot of this page. Deletion is carried out without asking for a reason

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see, receive or store card details. The store's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals, not children. Although the calculations concern paediatric patients, no child's data is collected — the values entered are not identifiable and are not stored.

Data protection (LGPD / GDPR)

The app does not process personal data: there is no collection, storage, sharing or transfer to protect, and data-subject rights have no object in it — there is no record of you to retrieve or delete.

The optional mailing list described above is processing of personal data:

  • Controller: Amanda Machado
  • Legal basis: consent (LGPD art. 7, I; GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), given by submitting the form
  • Purposes: only the two stated in that section. The data is never sold, transferred or used for anything else
  • Your rights — access, rectification, erasure, portability and withdrawal of consent — are exercised through the contact at the foot of this page and honoured without requiring a reason

Device permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, health data or notifications.

Network use

The app works entirely offline. The internet is used only to:

  • validate a purchase with the store
  • download updates to the app itself

No clinical data is transmitted in either case.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this same address with a new date. If a change ever introduced data collection — which is not planned — it would be announced inside the app before taking effect.
