How to get help

Write to the address above. A reply may take a few days — this is one physician, who also sees patients.

To get it solved faster, say three things: which device you use, which version of the app (shown at the foot of the screen), and what you expected to happen.

⚠️ Do not send patient data. Names, chart numbers, dates of birth — none of it is needed to fix a software problem, and we do not want to receive it.

Frequently asked

How the free trial works

Seven days with the full calculator, counted from first use. No account, no card, and no automatic charge when it ends: the app simply asks whether you want to buy.

What it costs

A one-time purchase. Not a subscription, no renewal, no recurring charge. The price is shown by the store itself, in your own currency.

I bought it and lost access

The purchase screen has an "Already purchased — restore" button. It asks the store and gives the access back. It works on any device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

If that does not fix it, write and say which of the two stores you used.

I changed phones

The purchase lives in your store account, not on the device. Install the app on the new phone and tap restore.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us:

  • Apple: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>
  • Google Play: <https://play.google.com/store/account>

If you have trouble there, write — it can also be requested from our side.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every calculation happens on the device. The internet is used only to talk to the store and to download app updates.

What happens to what I type

Nothing leaves the phone and nothing is saved. The details are in the privacy policy.

About the calculator

⚠️ This is a decision-support tool for healthcare professionals. It does not replace clinical judgement, and responsibility for the prescription rests with whoever prescribes. Always check the numbers before prescribing.

Questions about a formula, a safety limit or an alert? Write. I will answer with the arithmetic and the reference behind it.
